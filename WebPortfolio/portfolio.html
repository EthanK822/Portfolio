(Unreal Engine 5) Virtual Reality Hunting Early Concept

(OLD PROJECT) I was working on a vr hunting game in Unreal Engine 5, within 10 hours of time spent I have made item holsters for the player to slot items, which also allows them to spawn in with items. I have also added bullet drop, this allows the bullet to fall at a certain speed the longer the distance it takes to hit something. I also have experience with particles as you can see from the very simple blood effects. Again this concept is very bare bones..

(Unreal Engine 5) Group Collection Command Based War Game

(OLD PROJECT) This project is similar to mobile game by Supercell called, "Squad Busters", but my take on the concept is more of a RPG approach. After roughly 2 weeks of progress the game has a AI Following system allowing the player to gather troops and rush the enemies, when AI die a very comical explosion particle, which was actual the first time I learned anything about the Niagara Particle System UE5 offers. Also shown within the trailer is interactive grass that currently just change the color of grass depending on where you (or anything with custom depth) are at. This will be updates to laying the grass down where the player is at to add more of a immersive and satisfying feel to the game. There is also a very simple NPC dialouge system which was made within 45 minutes. I spent a lot of time making AI and I got sucked into making a cute bunny that runs around and does nothing, but I'm quite happy with how it turned out. This project also allowed me to test out my 3D modeling skills in Blender and it didn't go great, but I did make the grass myself. Most of everything else is just 3D models from Sketchfab and animations from Mixamo. .

(Unity) Memory Racer Game Jam

A game where you have 20 seconds to remember how the track looks, take a lap around the track and make it to the finish line. The track goes invisible as soon as you start moving. You have to 20 seconds total to memorize the track and take a lap around it. This game was made with very skilled people in different positions. I was responsible for the programming aspects. I had to learn many ways of generating a race track, and I experimented with many pattern genration methods such as: Convex Hull, Voronoi, I even went as far as making our own track editor.
